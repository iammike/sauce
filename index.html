02 — Per hour

How much, how often?

How many carbs you need per hour depends mostly on how long you're out. These tiers follow the current guidance (Morton et al., 2026), which raised the old 90 g/hr ceiling to 90–120 for trained athletes. Pick the band that matches your effort and see how much mix it takes.

Bands assume moderate-to-hard endurance effort — sit toward the low end for easy riding.
